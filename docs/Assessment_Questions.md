# Project Overview
- What are the main features and functionalities of the software?
- What are the key technical and operational challenges faced in the development of this software?
# Tech Stack
- What programming languages and frameworks are being used in the project?
- Why was this specific tech stack chosen over alternatives?
- What are the key advantages and disadvantages of the chosen stack?
- What third-party tools or APIs does the project depend on, and how do they fit into the overall architecture?
- How will the tech stack scale with future updates or user growth?
# Target Audience
- Who is the primary target audience for this software (e.g., individuals, businesses, specific industries)?
- What are the key user personas that should be considered during development?
- How do you ensure accessibility and usability for the target audience?
- What are the primary pain points of the target audience, and how does the software address them?
# Market Analysis
- What is the market demand for this type of software, and how was this assessed?
- Who are the main competitors in the market, and what differentiates your project from them?
- What are the key features or innovations that give your software a competitive edge?
- What strategies will be used for marketing the software to the target audience?
- How will you measure the success of the software in the market?
# Scale
- What is the expected scale of the software in terms of users, data, and transactions?
- How will the system be designed to handle scaling over time?
